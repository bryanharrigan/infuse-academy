Insured Lives Impact Analysis

Total US payer lives declined by -1.31M (-0.42% QoQ), driven by losses in the Commercial channel, notably Summit Blue Shield in Pennsylvania (-204.9K) and Keystone BlueCare (-36.8K). Offsetting this, Managed Medicaid expanded by +99.2K lives. Alongside this, Clearpath Rx and Summit Blue Cross Blue Shield launched new Commercial plans adding +68.4K and +51.2K lives, while Crosswind PBM and Vantage Script terminated Commercial plans, removing -38.9K and -29.7K lives respectively. Clearpath Rx and Verity Rx saw notable market share gains, with Clearpath Rx rising 138 ranks (+111.7K lives, +196.34%) and Verity Rx up 114 ranks (+82.4K lives, +117.58%).
